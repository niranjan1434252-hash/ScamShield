Safety Tips ✨

🔐 Protect your OTP

Never share OTPs, passwords or recovery codes with callers or message senders.

🔗 Check before clicking

Inspect the domain carefully. A familiar logo doesn't prove a website is genuine.

💸 Avoid urgent payments

Verify unexpected payment requests using a trusted contact method.

📲 Verify the sender

Caller ID and sender names can be spoofed. Don't rely on them alone.

Learn About Scams

🎣 Phishing

Messages or sites that imitate trusted organizations to steal information.

🎁 Prize scams

Unexpected winnings that demand fees or sensitive details to claim a reward.

☎ Impersonation

Someone pretends to be a bank, official or support agent to pressure you.

💳 Payment scams

Fraudulent requests that trick you into sending money or approving a payment.
